# Product

## Vision
A trusted Vietnamese B2B marketplace — operated by **GPU Swap Vietnam** as intermediary and managed broker — that puts idle GPU infrastructure back to work. Scope: hardware **already in Vietnam**, domestic B2B transactions.

## Why it exists
- **GPU scarcity:** new data-centre GPUs have long lead times; small organisations struggle to get capacity.
- **Idle equipment:** ended projects and upgrades leave GPU servers depreciating unused.
- **Start-ups on a budget:** used equipment with clear checks is a practical path to compute.
- **Opaque resale:** informal resale lacks origin, ownership, customs and condition information.
(No unverified market statistics are published on the site.)

## Business model
- Platform connects sellers and buyers; does not buy, own, warehouse or resell, and never holds the purchase price.
- Seller listing fee: **US$100 equivalent per listing per month** (one listing = one separately advertised item or lot).
- Seller success commission: **5 %** of the qualifying sale price, unless an individually negotiated rate is recorded and accepted.
- Platform controls do not guarantee payment or prevent all off-platform transactions.

## Target users
Visitor · Buyer · Seller · Reviewer (compliance/operations) · Broker · Administrator.

## Main journeys
See `USER-JOURNEYS.md`: seller submits listing (A), buyer finds GPU and enquires through broker (B), admin reviews listing (C), swap proposal (D), partner contact (E), broker introduction & transaction (F).

## MVP scope
Marketplace, wizard, review & badges, broker cases with anonymity, introductions, inspections, agreements, listing fees, commission tracking, requests, swaps, contact/partner inbox, admin content/settings, bilingual UI, audit log, new data-centre GPU market information page.

## Not in the MVP (roadmap)
GPU cloud rental / capacity marketplace, cross-border listings, integrated payments, bank-supported escrow, financing, automated valuations, partner APIs. See `ROADMAP.md`.

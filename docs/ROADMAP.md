# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — B2B domestic marketplace (this MVP)
Listings, review & badges, broker-controlled anonymous cases, introductions, inspections, agreements, manual fee & commission tracking, requests, swaps, bilingual site, new data-centre GPU market information.

## Phase 2 — Trade-in and service partners
Contracted inspection, data-sanitisation, logistics and installation partners (listed only with written authorisation); structured trade-in valuations; partner portals; S3 storage, real email, e-invoicing integration.

## Phase 3 — GPU capacity marketplace / GPU cloud rental
Rent GPU capacity by the hour or month from surplus infrastructure in Vietnam (shown on the site as **Coming soon**, not operational). Requires capacity contracts, SLAs, metering, data-processing terms and export-control screening of end users.

## Phase 4 — Cross-border transactions (subject to legal review)
Only after counsel confirms import/export pathways (incl. used/refurbished restrictions from 2027), US EAR screening, and a compliant importer-of-record structure.

## Phase 5 — Bank-supported payment options (if authorised and integrated)
Escrow, split payments or financing only through a licensed, contracted partner; displayed only when configured and approved in `/admin/disclosure`.
